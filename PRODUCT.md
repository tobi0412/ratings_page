# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Players of a football team who rate their teammates' performances on match dates, and the team admin who creates sessions and manages users.

## Product Purpose
An interactive peer-voting dashboard and analytics tool for recreational football teams, allowing players to rate their peer performances across 4 dimensions (Technique, Physical, Attitude, Vision) and choose the match MVP, generating historic charts and team leaderboards.

## Brand Personality
Competitive, energetic, sports-analytic, bold, modern. Uses a stark dark-green sports aesthetic that resembles a night soccer pitch under floodlights.

## Anti-references
Boring enterprise SaaS interfaces with flat pastel colors, generic light-themed dashboard layouts, or over-rounded childish card shapes.

## Design Principles
- **Pitch-Side Energy**: Use high-contrast lime accents, dark pitch-green surfaces, and bold athletic typography to evoke a real stadium vibe.
- **Micro-Interactiveness**: Rating sliders and buttons must respond with crisp transitions, hover glow states, and fluid Framer Motion animations.
- **Data Clarity & Depth**: Soccer statistics, line charts, and rankings must be visually parsed easily at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High-contrast text on dark backgrounds (adhering to WCAG guidelines).
- Accessible form controls and range inputs with clear visual focus rings.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion CSS variables and animations.
